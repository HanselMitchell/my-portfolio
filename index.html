01 / About

What I work on

Most of my work sits at the hardware layer of IoT systems: sensor front-ends, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi firmware, and the boards they run on. I follow it all the way to the cloud database and the screen someone actually reads.

I start projects from problems I can measure. Reservoir sedimentation surveys that need a manned boat and a crew. IV bags that run dry between nurse rounds. Students who can't name the dances or stories of their own province. If I can't put a number on the result, I keep testing until I can.

I study Computer Engineering at BINUS University as a recipient of the Widia Scholarship and Beasiswa Indonesia Maju (BIM). Outside class I assemble competition hardware for IEEE BINUS, support research at BINUS Maker Innovation Space, and lead BINUS Robotics & AI Network, which I founded in January 2026.

  • Embedded systems

    ESP32, ESP32-C3 and Raspberry Pi firmware in C++ and Python.

  • IoT & telemetry

    Multi-sensor fusion, GSM and Wi-Fi uplink, real-time Firebase sync.

  • PCB design & assembly

    Schematic, layout, SMD reflow and hand soldering of multi-layer boards.

  • AI integration

    LLM APIs and speech pipelines running on low-power hardware.

  • Robotics

    Surface-vessel navigation using GPS, a digital compass and sonar.
